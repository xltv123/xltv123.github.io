Research

Accepted · TRB Annual Meeting 2027

Bridge Defect Scoper: Vertical Instantiation of Multi-Agent Framework for Controlled and Traceable Bridge Assessment

Junjie Ma, Duoduo Liao

Question. Can an AI system combine a defect seen in an inspection image with engineering guidance and bridge records, and then show which evidence supports each claim in its report?

System. Built on the AgentMaster planning–execution–reporting architecture, a planner routes each task to specialized components: YOLO-based visual defect detection, retrieval from engineering references, text-to-SQL queries over bridge databases, and risk assessment. A reporting agent assembles the evidence from those outputs.

My role. Collected and prepared the data, developed and tuned the visual defect models, co-designed and implemented the system, and co-analyzed results and co-wrote the manuscript.

91.36%end-to-end report score on a 50-task benchmark
94.0%text-to-SQL accuracy with routing (87.2% standalone)
0.495mAP@0.5 on a four-class bridge defect dataset

Next. Hold out entire structures and capture conditions during evaluation, measure small-defect recall at fixed precision, and test whether each report claim can be traced to its supporting image region, query result, or source passage.
